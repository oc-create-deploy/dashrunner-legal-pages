DashRunner Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

DashRunner is an arcade game. This privacy policy explains the general data practices for the app.

Information We Collect

DashRunner does not require account registration. The app may store gameplay preferences or scores locally on your device. If online score or advertising features are enabled, standard technical information may be processed by the relevant service providers to operate those features.

Advertising and Analytics

The app may use advertising services such as Google Mobile Ads. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, approximate location, app interaction data, and diagnostic information according to their own privacy policies and platform settings.

Children

DashRunner is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been collected in error, please contact the developer through the App Store support contact.

Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device unless you delete the app or clear its data. Data handled by third-party services is retained according to those services' policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the App Store support contact for DashRunner.
